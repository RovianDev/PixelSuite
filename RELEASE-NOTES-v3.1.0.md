# Pixel Suite 3.1.0

**Pixel Suite now speaks 14 languages**, and brings Backup & Restore for held-back apps, new button actions, a new icon and, for patrons, Chrome+, Gboard+ and a built-in VPN.

---

## ✨ Highlights

- 💾 **Backup & Restore** for held-back apps, with their data
- 🌍 **14 languages**, this changelog included
- 🎮 **Pixel Stack!**, a little hidden game
- 🔘 **New button actions** for the power and volume buttons
- 🧹 **Hide the search bar** with one hold
- 💎 **Chrome+, Gboard+ and a built-in VPN** for patrons

---

## 💾 Backup & Restore

- 🛡️ **Held-back apps are saved before they update**, with their data (sign-ins, chats, settings and files) or the app only.
- 🗂️ **The 3 newest versions** of each app are kept, even after you uninstall it.
- 📋 **Back up any app yourself**, the app only or with all its data, and restore it whenever you like. Tap the **green button** to put a saved version back.

*In Protection › OTA & Store blocker › Play Store blocker.*

---

## 🌍 14 languages

- 🗣️ Pixel Suite now speaks **Chinese, Hindi, Spanish, Arabic, French, Portuguese, Russian, Bengali, Indonesian, German, Japanese, Turkish, Persian and Hebrew**, this changelog included.
- 📍 It follows your phone's language, or tap the **language pill** at the top right of the home page to pick one.
- ↔️ Arabic, Persian and Hebrew read right to left.

---

## 🎮 Pixel Stack!

- 🧱 **Hold the logo** at the top of Pixel Suite's home page for a little hidden game.
- 🎯 Stack the blocks as high as you can, and land them exactly for **Perfect!**
- 📣 **Share your score** with our Telegram community!

---

## 🔘 Buttons

- 🔘 **Hold power button**: open Gemini or Circle to Search, take a screenshot, or switch a quiet mode on and off: Silence, Do Not Disturb, Do Not Annoy, or No Annoyance for all three.
- 🔒 **Hold power button when locked**: pick a different action for when your phone is locked: Gemini (stock), Silence, Do Not Disturb, Do Not Annoy or No Annoyance. It works on its own, apart from Hold power button.
- 📷 **Double-press power button**: now you can also choose Camera (stock).
- 🔦 **Double-press volume down**: now you can also turn the flashlight on or off.

*In Shortcuts › Buttons.*

---

## ✨ Also new

- 🧹 **Hide search bar**: simply hold the search bar and choose **Hide widget**. The dock moves down, your apps use the space, and search stays in the app drawer. *(or Personalize › Pixel Launcher)*
- 🕒 **Always-on display**: keep it on while charging, and let the power button show it for a few seconds when you turn the screen off. *(Personalize › Always-on display)*
- 🔢 **Auto-confirm 4-digit PIN**: your phone unlocks as soon as you type the 4th digit, no Enter needed. *(Protection › Privacy & Security › Lock screen)*
- 🔎 **Search in Chrome**: select text in any app, tap ⋮ and choose **Search in Chrome**. The words open straight into Chrome.
- 🗑️ **Only Clear all**: recents shows just Clear all, in the middle, without Select and Screenshot. *(Personalize › Pixel Launcher › Recent Apps)*
- 🎨 **New Pixel Suite icon**: it fills the circle edge to edge, without the white plate, and has a themed version that follows your wallpaper colours.

## 💎 Patreon

### 🚀 Chrome+

- 🚀 **Controls at the bottom**, with smoother hand-crafted animations for tabs, the tab switcher and new tabs.
- 🕶️ **Incognito in one tap**: the orange + in the tab switcher opens an incognito tab.
- 🧩 **Extensions**: VPN, Ad Blocker and Dark pages in one sheet, from the puzzle button in Chrome's toolbar. Tap one to use it, or hold it to pin it to the toolbar.
- 🛡️ **Ad Blocker**: pick a filtering level for each website. Complete also hides cookie banners and pop-ups, and you can remove any part of a page yourself. Its switch in the Extensions sheet turns it off or on for every website.
- 🌙 **Dark pages**: websites open dark with no white flash, or pure black with Super Dark: always, with your phone's dark mode, or on a schedule. Pictures and videos keep their colours.
- ⭐ **Speed dial**: round icons for up to 19 websites on Chrome's new tab page. Drag to move them, or drop one on another to make a folder that follows Super Dark. Give any website your own round picture.

*In Patreon › Google apps › Chrome+.*

### 🌐 VPN

- 🌍 **Browse from another country** on free servers worldwide, with the fastest server first.
- 📱 **For the whole phone or Chrome only.** Chrome's location follows the VPN.

*In Patreon › VPN, or from its Quick Settings tile.*

### ⌨️ Gboard+

- ⌨️ **Your own toolbar**: up to 7 shortcuts, like Undo, Redo, Quick Select, Select all, Cut, Copy and Paste, in every app. Select all and Quick Select open the Copy and Paste menu right away.
- 🔤 **Rounded keys** with smooth pop-ups. Swipe left from delete to remove letter by letter.
- ↕️ **Resize or move the keyboard.** With Gboard's number row on, the symbols page keeps the same key size.
- ✍️ **Auto-correction off** for the languages you choose.
- 📎 **Keep every clip** in the clipboard.
- 🎙️ **Voice typing with checkmark**: speak, then tap the checkmark to add your words.
- 🧊 **Frosted Gboard**: the keyboard's background turns to frosted glass: what's behind it shows through, blurred, with the glass rims, in light, dark and Super Dark. The keys stay as they are.

*In Patreon › Google apps › Gboard+.*

### 📞 Google dialer+

- 🎙️ **Recording quality**: Standard for smaller files, HD for a clearer voice (now the default), or Lossless for the clearest.

*In Patreon › Google apps › Google dialer+.*

## 🪄 Polish

- 🌑 **Super Dark**: the menu over selected text (Cut, Copy, Search in Chrome and more) is now black with a rim in every app, and Chrome's incognito stays pure black everywhere, also while switching tabs.
- ⚡ **Options apply on their own**: Super Dark-Mode and the surface style change as you switch them, other options restart their own app, and the Refresh pill only shows for Pixel Launcher options.
- 🔄 **Reboot pill**: some features only start working after a reboot. After you update, a Reboot pill shows until your phone restarts. Tap it to see which features need the reboot, then tap again to reboot.
- 🌄 **Google feed**: swipe right on the home screen and the feed slides in over your wallpaper, with no white panel flashing first.
- 🏠 **Pixel Launcher**: holding the search bar now shows only Widget settings and Hide widget.

## 🐞 Bug fixes

### 🏠 Pixel Launcher

- 🧠 **No more slowdowns**: after many switches between light and dark (a dark mode schedule, say), it could slow down, stop responding and restart. It stays smooth now.
- 🖐️ **App menus**: holding an app at some home screen spots showed only a few small icons, and its menu vanished. Menus open fully everywhere now.
- 🔴 **Folder dots**: in the app drawer, folder notification dots floated above their folders on some Pixel Launcher versions. They sit on the folders again.
- 🪟 **Frosted Glass menus**: the row of icons at the top of an app's menu showed before the rest. It fades in with the menu now.

### 📱 Navigation buttons

- 🏠 **Swipe home**: after you turned the screen, swiping up to go home could stop working when the Back button was on. It works every time now.
- 🧭 **Bottom menus**: some apps put their bottom menu under the buttons. It sits above them now.

*In Shortcuts › Gestures › Navigation buttons.*

### 🌐 Chrome

- 🛟 **Ad Blocker**: Chrome could close by itself if you left a page or closed a tab while it was still loading. It stays open now.
- 📰 **Chrome feed**: the feed could jump while sponsored stories were hidden. It stays put now and scrolls more smoothly.

### ⌨️ Keyboard & Super Dark

- #️⃣ **Number keys**: in Super Dark, Gboard's number keys (for PINs, phone numbers and amounts) were black on black and didn't light up when tapped. They show and light up again.
- ➗ **Gboard+ numbers page**: the + - * / column now matches the other keys. The space and delete keys look like keys again and no longer disappear in dark mode.
- 🔔 **Notifications**: in Super Dark, one-line notifications at the bottom of the shade stayed grey. They're Super Dark now.
- ✅ **Select apps**: the keyboard stayed open over the list after you ticked an app from search. It closes now.
