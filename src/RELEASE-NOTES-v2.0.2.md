# Pixel Suite v2.0.2

Themes with Super Dark-Mode, smarter folder moving in the app drawer, and a restart prompt in the app.

## 🌑 Super Dark-Mode (new Themes section)
- A true-black dark theme for OLED screens, under Pixel Launcher in the Pixel Suite app.
- Settings, the shade, quick tiles, notifications, the lock screen PIN/pattern screen, the app drawer, folders, Gboard, the Google app and the search bar go black. Cards and menus sit a few steps above black so they stay apart; brighter accent colors keep their color.
- Follows light and dark: the quick settings tile or a dark-theme schedule switching to light brings back the normal light theme, and going dark brings Super Dark back.
- Turns window blur off while it is in effect (solid black panels, less GPU work) and brings it back in light theme or when switched off.
- Applies by itself after a reboot and works live when switched.
- Folder menus, app shortcut menus and launcher dialogs are solid while blur is off, matching the folder background under Super Dark. App shortcut menus follow Super Dark even when other launcher customizations are off.

## App drawer folders
- Moving a folder pushes the other folders aside, like apps in a folder; folders stay together in their own rows.
- No more jumping or shaking when a folder is dropped.
- Dragging a whole folder to the home screen shows Cancel instead of Remove.

## Folder menus and drag buttons
- Sort in the held drawer folder menu folds open Auto (A–Z) and Custom with new icons, like the folder ⋮ menu.
- Menus fit their longest label; no stutter when a folder menu closes.
- Remove, Cancel and Uninstall fade in after a drag starts, so they are never cut off.

## Pixel Suite app
- A Restart pill appears after changing a setting that needs Pixel Launcher or Files to restart.

## Setup
Update and reboot. Keep the recommended LSPosed scope; for Super Dark-Mode also tick **Gboard**, **Google** and **Settings** (now recommended).
Tested on Pixel 11 Pro, Android 17, Pixel Launcher 17.
