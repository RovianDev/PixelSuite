## Pixel Suite 2.0.6

🔒 **Privacy & Security**

• Lock apps and folders with your fingerprint or PIN.

• Locked apps can’t be uninstalled, disabled or paused without unlocking, and locked folders can’t be removed.

• Locked apps show a blurred preview in recents.

• Relock instantly when you leave, or keep apps unlocked for 1 minute.

• A padlock badge marks locked apps and folders on the home screen, in the drawer and in Google search.

🗂️ **Recents**

• Lock a card from its menu so swiping and Clear all leave it alone. In split screen, you can lock each app on its own.

• In landscape, app names stay centred above their cards and the status bar fades out.

⚙️ **Miscellaneous** (all off by default)

• **Faster screenshots:** screenshots appear instantly from the buttons or three fingers.

• **Screenshots of secure screens:** capture and record apps that normally block it.

• **Hide accessibility from apps:** pick which apps can’t see your accessibility services.

• **Keyboard bar:** hide the globe and move the dismiss arrow to the right.

• **Hide Developer Options:** apps can’t tell Developer options or USB debugging are on. Tap the gear to open Developer options.

🎨 **Improvements**

• **Change icon:** move and zoom your image before applying it.

• Icons and folders follow your icon shape everywhere, including Google search.

• Folders resize to fit what’s inside (4×4 from 10 apps, 5×5 from 17), and can hold a single app.

• Smoother split-screen previews and Clear all animation in recents.

• Long-press menus always open next to the app, never on top of it.

• The Pixel Suite app now follows Super Dark.

🐛 **Fixes**

• Apps disappearing after a move, outdated folder previews and wrong drop targets.

• Icon reset, custom icon edges, and themed icons flashing their original colours.

• The “Item removed” pill in the wrong place, and missing Super Dark in search and uninstall dialogs.

Install over the current official build, then reboot.
