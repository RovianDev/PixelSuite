# Pixel Suite 3.1.2

**Two new games land in Pixel Arcade!** Plus a reworked Pixel Stack, Hide At a Glance, new Navigation buttons options, and fixes.

---

## ✨ Highlights

- 🕹️ **Pixel Arcade**: two new games, Pixel Blast and Pixel Siege, and a reworked Pixel Stack
- ⏸️ **Pause and continue** any game, even after a restart
- 🧹 **Hide At a Glance** on Android 16 and older
- 🧭 **Navigation buttons**: choose their distance and hide the gesture bar

---

## 🕹️ Pixel Arcade: two more games added!

- **Hold the logo** at the top of Pixel Suite's home page and pick your game. Every card shows its game playing live!
- **Pixel Blast (new!)**: bomb your way through crates and ghosts! Kick bombs, grab gems, take down a boss every 5 levels, and turn Evil or Angelic, each with wild powers of its own.
- **Pixel Siege (new!)**: defend the logo with your tank! It grows through five levels, a boss rolls in every 5 levels, and legendary prizes like Airstrike, Shockwave and the Nuke wait on buttons above your controls.
- **Pixel Stack, reworked!** Three hearts, golden blocks, a faster gear every 100 points, and every block plays the next note of a piano tune.
- **Pause and continue**: leave any time and pick up right where you stopped, even after a restart. Backups keep your best scores and paused games.
- **Show off your score!** Each game has its own score card for our Telegram community.

---

## ✨ New

- 🧹 **Hide At a Glance** (Android 16 and older): removes At a Glance from the home screen and frees its row for your apps, folders and widgets. (Pixel Launcher)
- 🧭 **Navigation buttons**: choose how far the buttons sit from the gesture bar, and hide the gesture bar while its swipes keep working. (Gestures › Navigation buttons)

---

## 💎 Patreon

- 🌙 **Dark pages**: card numbers are readable on payment pages that forced dark text.

*In Chrome+ › Extensions.*

---

## 🪄 Polish

- 🔠 **Persian**: Pixel Suite now uses the Vazirmatn font, with more natural wording throughout.
- ✂️ **Free screenshot crop and Pixel Studio doodles**: greyed out, with a note, on phones without the screenshot editor (Pixel 9 and newer have it).
- 🏷️ **LSPosed scope rows**: app names stay as LSPosed shows them, in every language.

---

## 🐞 Bug fixes

- 🧭 **Navigation buttons**: Pixel Launcher could crash on newer versions with the buttons on, and the buttons stayed on screen in a full-screen landscape video.
- 🗂️ **App drawer**: an app dragged out of an open folder could still show in the old folder.
- 📦 **Android 16's Pixel Launcher**: moving several apps said "No room on this page", and App drawer folders didn't work.
- 🔢 **Auto-confirm 4-digit PIN**: it now works on the newer lock screen.
- 🔍 **Hide search bar**: it works on more Pixel Launcher versions that said it isn't supported.

---

## 📥 Install

1. Install **PixelSuite-v3.1.2.apk** over your current version.
2. Reboot (the Reboot pill in Pixel Suite does it for you).
