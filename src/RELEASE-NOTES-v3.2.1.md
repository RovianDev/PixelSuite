Full support for the November update, Pixel search, Pixel dialer, Pixel PDF, a home screen folder menu, Force stop, Phone check, and lots of fixes.

## 📱 November update

- **Android 17 QPR2 (November update)**: Pixel Suite works on the new build, and older builds keep working as before. The update broke the features below, and they all work again:
- **Super Dark**: true black again in the notification shade, app drawer, folders and everywhere else it applies, also after a restart, with no grey screen while unlocking. (Themes › Super Dark-Mode)
- **App menus**: holding an app on the home screen or in the app drawer opens its menu every time, as one frosted panel with its rim, its shortcuts and Pixel Suite's options.
- **Folders**: home screen folders open with the same frosted look, blur and rim as the app drawer's, and the effect when you remove a folder is back.
- **Several icons at once**: dropping several selected icons on Remove removes them all (Undo brings them all back), and moving several to another page no longer leaves the originals behind.
- **Screenshots**: the shutter sound plays again, following your ring volume.
- **App & folder lock**: a locked app asks for your fingerprint before it opens instead of after, and removing a locked app or folder from the home screen asks for your fingerprint again.
- **Double tap to sleep**: double tapping the alarm, weather or date on the lock screen turns the screen off instead of opening them, and tapping your PIN no longer turns the screen off. (Gestures › Tap or Double-tap to wake › Lock-screen behavior › Pixel Suite)
- **Screen recording panel**: opens cleanly, without Quick Settings showing through, a flash or a pale card, and its buttons sit in place.
- **Share menu**: hidden sharing apps stay hidden again, and the Edit button is back when you share from Google Photos.
- **Toasts**: "Video saved", "Video deleted" and other system toasts are one pill again and follow your theme, Super Dark included.
- **Also back**: the Pixel Suite row and its icon in the home screen's hold menu, the fingerprint prompt's style, icon shapes, Clear all for app pairs, and hiding shortcuts in the app drawer's menus.

## ✨ New

- **Fresh animation for Pixel Suite**: pages, dialogs and menus open and close with an all-new animation, much smoother and built with new techniques.
- **Phone check**: tap the **!** at the top left of the main page to see your phone, Android version and security update, and whether Pixel Suite is **Safe**, **Untested** or **Unsafe** on it, with quick links to OTA blocker and Telegram.
- **Home screen folder menu**: hold a folder to Rename it, Sort it (A–Z or your own order), Lock it or Remove it, with the same menu as the app drawer's folders.
- **Force stop**: hold any app and choose Force stop to close it completely.
- **Hide app shortcuts** on the home screen, in the app drawer, or both. (Pixel Launcher › App shortcuts)
- **Paused apps**: holding an app you paused opens its menu with its shortcuts.
- **Safe and Unsafe versions**: when an app has an update waiting, your installed version shows as Safe in green and the waiting one as Unsafe in red. Tap the safe version to download that exact version. (OTA & Store blocker › Play Store blocker)
- **Hide Apps list** from the menu you get when you hold an empty spot on the home screen. (Pixel Launcher › Hide Apps list)
- **Hide swipe-up hint**: no more hint after a few failed swipes to open the app drawer. (App drawer › Hide swipe-up hint)
- **Seconds** in the status bar clock. (Miscellaneous › Seconds in the status bar)
- **Fresh start**: on a new install every feature starts off, so you turn on only what you want. Updating keeps your settings as they are.

## 💎 Patreon

- **Pixel Suite apps**: Pixel search, Pixel dialer and Pixel PDF, which I've been working on for a long time, are finally here, all in one place. (Pixel Suite apps)
- **Pixel search**: completely replace the useless Google search bar with one that actually searches your phone. Apps, contacts, settings and files, all found on the phone (only web searches go online). It even finds words and numbers inside your PDFs and your Word, Excel and PowerPoint files. Hold the bar to change its size, colors, theme and buttons. (Pixel Suite apps › Pixel search)
- **Pixel search, everything inside**: Favorites and favorite contacts behind the heart, currency conversion with flags, a calculator that also converts units (like 5 km in miles), featured apps and recent searches, pins and a search engine button on the bar, your keyboard's word suggestions, closing animations you pick from a live preview, and a home screen widget. Files show when they were last changed and appear as soon as they're added; hold one to share it, show it in its folder or delete it.
- **Pixel search in the Widgets feed**: the two work together. Pull down in the Widgets feed and Pixel search opens over the blurred feed. (Widgets feed › Pixel search) It's on the lock screen too, where calculations and currency conversions work without unlocking. (Widgets feed › arrow next to Pixel search › Search on the lock screen)
- **Pixel dialer**: a full phone app made for Pixel Suite. It records every call automatically, both sides, in the quality you choose, and **records WhatsApp voice and video calls** too, saved as a video of the call with both sides' sound. Notes you type during a call are saved to Google Keep, one note per person. It has Favorites, Calls, Contacts and Recordings tabs, lets you answer with a tap or a swipe, and has the Pixel Suite look, Super Dark included. (Pixel Suite apps › Pixel dialer) ***(Recording someone without their knowledge can be against the law where you live. You are responsible for your recordings and how you use them; RovianDev is not.)***
- **Pixel PDF**: a full PDF app. Scan documents and ID cards, turn images, Word and PowerPoint files into PDFs, and PDFs into Word, PowerPoint or JPG. Merge, compress, lock and unlock PDFs, add text, sign and draw on them, print them, and open PDFs from other apps. (Pixel Suite apps › Pixel PDF)
- **Lock screen Widgets feed** on the November update: the lock screen moves like a page, and you can swipe the feed open from almost anywhere, even below a notification, without pulling down the shade or setting off the PIN pad, the back arrow or the fingerprint sensor. It bounces like the home screen when it settles and when you swipe past the widgets. (Widgets feed)
- **Lock screen and fingerprint**: after you leave the Widgets feed, the fingerprint icon shows at once (never a padlock) and your fingerprint unlocks right away. Touching the fingerprint spot right after using the feed doesn't unlock by mistake.

## 🛠️ Patreon fixes

- **Lock screen Widgets feed**: with the PIN pad up, swiping sideways no longer slides the feed in behind it, and the feed no longer locks the phone again right after you unlock with your fingerprint.
- **Gboard+**: tapping the clipboard icon works again right after you copy something, the keys come back after closing the tools panel, voice typing works in Arabic and other languages and no longer leaves the toolbar empty and blue, swipes no longer press toolbar buttons or block the back gesture, and the floating keyboard keeps its layout, also at the top of the screen, with rounded corners and a toolbar that fills it.

## 🐞 Pixel Suite fixes

- **Autofill OTP**: the code sits at the start of the keyboard's suggestion row, never at the top of the screen or over Google's own code suggestion. It works with the floating keyboard, goes away once the code is typed, and codes made only of letters are found too.
- **Share to Telegram** is back in Pixel Stack, Pixel Blast and Pixel Siege: after a game, share your score to the Telegram group.
- **Lock screen**: a sideways or diagonal swipe no longer pulls the notification shade down for a moment.
- **PIN pad**: swiping on a number no longer leaves it highlighted.
- **App drawer folders**: the hold menu sits right next to a folder, even near the bottom of the drawer.
- **Do Not Annoy**: "Couldn't reach your watch" no longer shows without a Pixel Watch with LTE, or while that option is off.
