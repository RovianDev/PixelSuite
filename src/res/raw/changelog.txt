## v3.2.0
Widgets feed, Autofill OTP, Block WhatsApp calls, a much bigger Pixel Arcade, feature films, and lots of fixes.

✨ New
• **Autofill OTP**: one-time codes from SMS, chat apps and email (Outlook included) show above your keyboard and fill in with a tap, or by themselves if you turn that on. Codes with letters and digits work too. (Autofill OTP)
• **Block WhatsApp calls**: while Do Not Annoy is on, WhatsApp calls never ring and callers see Unable to call. A notification shows who called, with a Call button to call them back. Blocked calls still show in WhatsApp's Calls tab, and messages and your own calls work as usual. (Do Not Annoy)
• **USB on top**: the USB notification stays pinned at the top and picks File transfer when your phone is unlocked, with USB debugging right under it. (Miscellaneous › USB on top)
• **Quick USB modes**: switch to File transfer without your fingerprint or PIN, as long as your phone is unlocked. (Miscellaneous › USB on top)
• **Safe from updates**: an option that works inside an app (like Block WhatsApp calls) keeps that app from updating while it's on, so an update can't break it. (OTA & Store blocker › Play Store blocker)
• **App lists**: lists where you pick apps now have a proper search bar and a Select all box.
• **LSPosed scope**: External Storage and WhatsApp now show as recommended, since Files and Block WhatsApp calls need them.
• **On its side**: Pixel Suite's pages now fit a phone turned on its side.
• **Feature films**: every feature page now opens with a short film that shows what it does.

💎 Patreon
• **Widgets feed**: your own page of widgets next to the home screen and the lock screen. Add widgets from the usual picker, move and resize them, and pick its side and how see-through it is. Keep the Google feed on the other side, or leave it out. On the lock screen, widgets can open the apps you pick without unlocking, so choose carefully. (Widgets feed)
• **Chrome+ tabs**: opening a tab always zooms smoothly now instead of fading, and going back to the tab grid, the tab keeps its whole frame as it shrinks into place, at full frame rate. The colours follow the tab, and your current tab's card is never cut off by the bottom bar. (Chrome+)
• **Chrome+ back and forward**: the arrows on the bottom bar follow the page's history, so a dim arrow means there's nowhere to go. (Chrome+)
• **Chrome+ tab switcher**: more space between the plus buttons and the Tabs group on the bottom bar. (Chrome+)
• **Gboard+ Arabic keys**: the letters ر and ى now trade places on the third row for easier typing, as a Patreon member asked. (Gboard+)

🛠️ Patreon fixes
• **Chrome+**: the pinned extension button no longer disappears while a page scrolls, the loading circle no longer sits in the new tab page's feed, and the bottom bar stays down on the inactive tabs page, and a site opened from a speed dial folder opens in the same tab again, so back takes you to the new tab page instead of leaving Chrome.
• **Gboard+**: codes, autofill and copied text show above the keyboard again with Hide suggestion chips on. The toolbar shortcuts no longer stop answering taps, "Clear the clipboard?" no longer sends the keyboard away in Chrome, the copied-text chip no longer runs off the edge, and the clipboard's back arrow is easier to tap.

🕹️ Pixel Arcade
• **Landscape support**: all three games work in landscape, and side swipes no longer close a game by accident.
• **Small screens**: game cards now fit on smaller screens and with large text.
• **Angelic or Demonic** (Pixel Blast): choose a side at the altars and level it up to 10. Angelic laser eyes and Demonic fire breath one-shot more ghosts with every level and every ghost at level 10, hit bosses hard and break through pillars (the laser shatters them, the fire melts them), growing stronger and cooler as you go.
• **Thunder or Inferno** (Pixel Siege): fit a core after a stage and level it up to 10. Thunder's lightning and Inferno's rockets one-shot more tanks with every level and every tank at level 10 (bosses and warlords aside), and your helper drone and overshield change with your way.
• **100 stages**: a new named boss every 5 stages and more unique enemies the further you go. Pixel Siege also gets doors between stages, redrawn enemy tanks and 14 fields with fuel drums.
• **Pixel Siege base**: it gets tougher as you level up, with a health bar, steel corners and a police light when it's hit.
• **Full screen**: the status bar and home bar hide while you play, and leaving takes a second swipe so you don't exit by accident. The games menu keeps both bars.
• **Pause and Exit buttons**, damage numbers over every hit and level-up titles. Tapping the field now only picks up power-ups.
• **New menu**: the games menu bounces on springs, and each game plays its own note as your finger passes over it. The bubble you hold is rounder and bigger, shakes, and has new sounds.
• **Fixes**: you can come back after losing a life, paused games no longer open blank, every stage has its ghosts and every boss stage its boss, ghosts no longer all look and move alike in later stages, both fingers always count on the controls, a boss no longer takes a hit from every overlapping blast at once, and a new stage no longer starts with you moving or shooting on your own.

🪄 Polish
• **Unique icons**: rows that shared an icon now each have their own.

🐞 Pixel Suite fixes
• **Navigation buttons**: the keyboard no longer covers what you type in some apps, like Instagram's comments.
• **Reduce screenshot delay**: it no longer crashes System UI's screenshot process when System UI isn't ticked in Pixel Suite's scope.
• **Double-press volume down**: only a real call stops it now, not an app that leaves the phone in call mode or paused audio.
• **Files › Newest first**: going back into a folder no longer shows it empty.
• **Search**: long results now end at a whole word instead of being cut off mid-word.

🔧 Google fixes
• **Files**: folders with thousands of files (like a big Download or Camera folder) now open in about a second instead of staying empty. Tick External Storage in Pixel Suite's LSPosed scope for this.
• **Google feed**: swiping to it right after closing it no longer does nothing. It opens and follows your finger.
• **Google feed**: a swipe from the edge no longer closes it as a back gesture, and right after it closes, the back arrow no longer shows up and does nothing.

Install over your current version and reboot.

## v3.1.2
Two new games land in Pixel Arcade! Plus a reworked Pixel Stack, Hide At a Glance, new Navigation buttons options, and fixes.

🕹️ Pixel Arcade: two more games added!
• **Hold the logo** at the top of Pixel Suite's home page and pick your game. Every card shows its game playing live!
• **Pixel Blast (new!)**: bomb your way through crates and ghosts! Kick bombs, grab gems, take down a boss every 5 levels, and turn Evil or Angelic, each with wild powers of its own.
• **Pixel Siege (new!)**: defend the logo with your tank! It grows through five levels, a boss rolls in every 5 levels, and legendary prizes like Airstrike, Shockwave and the Nuke wait on buttons above your controls.
• **Pixel Stack, reworked!** Three hearts, golden blocks, a faster gear every 100 points, and every block plays the next note of a piano tune.
• **Pause and continue**: leave any time and pick up right where you stopped, even after a restart. Backups keep your best scores and paused games.
• **Show off your score!** Each game has its own score card for our Telegram community.

✨ New
• 🧹 **Hide At a Glance** (Android 16 and older): removes At a Glance from the home screen and frees its row for your apps, folders and widgets. (Pixel Launcher)
• 🧭 **Navigation buttons**: choose how far the buttons sit from the gesture bar, and hide the gesture bar while its swipes keep working. (Gestures › Navigation buttons)

💎 Patreon
• 🌙 **Dark pages**: card numbers are readable on payment pages that forced dark text.

🪄 Polish
• 🔠 **Persian**: Pixel Suite now uses the Vazirmatn font, with more natural wording throughout.
• ✂️ **Free screenshot crop and Pixel Studio doodles**: greyed out, with a note, on phones without the screenshot editor (Pixel 9 and newer have it).
• 🏷️ **LSPosed scope rows**: app names stay as LSPosed shows them, in every language.

🐞 Bug fixes
• 🧭 **Navigation buttons**: Pixel Launcher could crash on newer versions with the buttons on, and the buttons stayed on screen in a full-screen landscape video.
• 🗂️ **App drawer**: an app dragged out of an open folder could still show in the old folder.
• 📦 **Android 16's Pixel Launcher**: moving several apps said "No room on this page", and App drawer folders didn't work.
• 🔢 **Auto-confirm 4-digit PIN**: it now works on the newer lock screen.
• 🔍 **Hide search bar**: it works on more Pixel Launcher versions that said it isn't supported.

Install over your current version and reboot.

## v3.1.1
Turn off system-wide ad blocking, Hide search bar on more launchers, longer names on Chrome's speed dial, and fixes.

💎 Patreon
• 🛡️ **Block ads in every app**: turn it off to block ads only in Chrome and Google News. Your phone's hosts file goes back to stock right away, and removing the ad blocker module in your root manager now turns it off too. (Patreon › Google apps)
• ⭐ **Speed dial**: website and folder names now use their whole column, so long names show much more before the "…".

🪄 Polish
• 🗂️ **App drawer**: a folder or app you drop back in its own spot now lands with the same little rattle as a move.

🐞 Bug fixes
• 🔢 **Lock screen**: typing your PIN after a restart could turn the screen off. It stays on now.
• 🧹 **Hide search bar**: older Pixel Launcher versions said it isn't supported. It works there now.
• 🧭 **Navigation buttons**: an attempt to fix a second pair of buttons showing at the top of the screen on some phones.
• 📁 **Speed dial**: long folder names lost their first letters. They show from the start now.

Install over your current version and reboot.
